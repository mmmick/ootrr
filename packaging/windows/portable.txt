OOTRR portable

This file turns on portable mode. Nothing is installed and nothing is written
to the registry. OOTRR keeps its caches in the "data" folder next to
ootrr.exe instead of in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ootrr.

To start: double-click ootrr.exe. A console window opens and OOTRR opens in
your browser. Keep the window open while you use it, and close it to stop.

To remove OOTRR: delete this folder.

Delete this file if you would rather keep the data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ootrr.
Your settings live in the browser, so they are the same either way.

To check your download, compare the zip's hash with SHA256SUMS.txt on the
release page. In PowerShell:
  Get-FileHash .\OOTRR-<version>-windows-x64-portable.zip
